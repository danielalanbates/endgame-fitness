## Trademark Analysis

### Strengths (Trademarkability)

✅ **Reversed phrase makes it more distinctive**
- Not the common "Healthy Mind, Healthy Body"
- Shows creativity and unique branding

✅ **Descriptive but potentially protectable**
- Describes what you do (fitness → mental health)
- With consistent use, could acquire "secondary meaning"
- Stronger when combined with logo/design

✅ **No exact matches found in preliminary search**
- App Store: Clear
- Suggests low conflict risk

### Weaknesses (Potential Issues)

⚠️ **Somewhat descriptive**
- Describes the benefits of your service
- May face scrutiny from USPTO if too generic

⚠️ **Common individual words**
- "Healthy," "Body," "Mind" are all common
- Combined phrase is more distinctive

⚠️ **Related to famous quote**
- Inversion of well-known phrase
- Could be seen as derivative

## Recommendations

### Immediate Actions

1. **✅ USE THE NAME** for development/testing
   - Low risk for internal use
   - Name appears available

2. **🔍 VERIFY TRADEMARK** before launch:
   - Hire trademark attorney for comprehensive search
   - Cost: $500-$1,500 for professional search
   - Worth it before investing in branding

3. **📝 FILE TRADEMARK APPLICATION** (if clear):

   - Establishes priority date
