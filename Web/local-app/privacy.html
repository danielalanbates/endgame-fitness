Your training. Your data.

Updated November 18, 2026 · Public beta

BatesAI account sign-in

Endgame uses the existing BatesAI account system. You can sign in with Google or Apple. Those providers authenticate you; Endgame does not receive your provider password or access your contacts, posts or social graph. A secure BatesAI session connects your Endgame profile to the same account you use across BatesAI apps.

What Endgame stores

The web app stores your display name, goals, equipment, preferred schedule, age, height, body weight, experience, five-rep strength assessment, training logs, active workout and quest progress in your browser and in a private account record on BatesAI’s Cloudflare infrastructure. The shared account session is held in a secure, HttpOnly cookie and expires after 30 days.

Friends and visibility

Your friend code lets people request a connection. Both sides must accept before aggregate statistics (display name, level, sessions, sets and weekly training days) become visible. Detailed exercises, loads and training preferences are not shared through the party interface. Remove a friend in Your party to stop sharing.

Mac app

The Mac beta signs in to the same BatesAI account and stores its session in Keychain. Native training data stays on your Mac. Web and Mac training logs currently use different formats and do not sync with each other. Your existing local player and onboarding answers stay on the Mac when you connect your BatesAI account.

Backups and retention

You can export a web backup from Settings. It contains personal training data: keep it private. Cloud data stays until you delete your Endgame profile. Device backups remain on the device until you clear site data. Signing out revokes the current BatesAI session. Other sessions expire within 30 days.

Delete your Endgame data

Sign in → Settings → Delete my Endgame account. Confirm the deletion to remove your cloud profile, training log and friendships. This also ends your current BatesAI session. Clear site data in your browser and delete exported files to remove your own device copies. The Mac beta’s local player deletion is separate from cloud account deletion.

If you cannot sign in, email help@batesai.org with your request. We will verify ownership before deleting records. Do not email passwords or access tokens.

No ads or automatic social posts

The app does not include advertising or analytics trackers. You choose whom to invite and what to share. Workout data is not sent to Google, Apple or social networks by Endgame.

Fitness guidance

Programs are general educational tools for adults, not medical care or a guarantee of results. Work within your ability. Stop if a movement causes pain and seek qualified help when needed.
