Endgame Fitness Privacy Policy

Applies to the Endgame Fitness RPG apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac and to batesai.org/fitness. Last updated October 27, 2026. Operated by Bates LLC (“we”).

Endgame Fitness is a training log with a game and social layer. This page explains exactly what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. The general BatesAI privacy policy also applies to your batesai.org membership account.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

  • Nothing is read until you turn on Health in the app and approve Apple’s permission sheet. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.
  • The app may read your steps and active energy to show them on your dashboard. Only today’s step count and today’s active calories are sent to batesai.org, so the “Step Count” quest can be completed. No other Health data is read.
  • The app may write the strength workouts you finish to Apple Health.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with data brokers or any third party, and never stored in iCloud by us.

Who can see what

  • You choose. New profiles start private; the profile setup screen and Account let you turn on “Public profile”. When it is on, your hero name, level, title, class, streak and workout count can appear on global leaderboards, in player search and on your public player card. Your email is never shown to other players.
  • Friends and guildmates see your activity feed items (for example “finished a workout”, a new personal record or a level-up) and can react and comment.
  • You can block any player. Blocked players and you stop seeing each other everywhere. You can report players, comments, feed items and guilds; reports go to help@batesai.org for review.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data and there are no ads or third-party analytics in the app. We use service providers only to run the service: Cloudflare (hosting and database), Apple and Google (only if you choose their sign-in), and, only when you ask the in-app AI coach to build or improve a program, OpenRouter, which receives the program request text (no account details, no Health data).

Deleting your data

In the app: Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your batesai.org membership account and everything listed above from our servers (guilds you own pass to another member). Workouts already written to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there. You can also email help@batesai.org.

Children

Endgame Fitness is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change. Questions: help@batesai.org · batesai.org.
